Design studio

Design your own merch on our blanks

Pick a garment, recolor it to any Pantone shade, then drop in your logo or add styled text until it looks right. Submit the design and we reply with pricing and a production proof. No account, no payment up front.

How the design studio works

  1. Pick a blank. Every garment in the studio is a real stocked style, photographed on a model, so what you design on is what gets produced.
  2. Recolor it. Choose a stocked colorway or push the garment to a Pantone shade. The mockup updates on the real photo, not a flat vector template.
  3. Add your artwork. Upload a PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, PDF or AI, or set arched and straight text in any built-in or uploaded font, then scale and place it on the front or back.
  4. Send it. Your mockup travels with the request. Hook onSubmit to your cart or quote form.
