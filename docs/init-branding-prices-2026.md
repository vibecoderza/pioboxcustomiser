**All prices ex VAT, apparel, and delivery.** These are decoration/print charges only — not blank cost, not sell price.
Oversized DTG: quote (40cm × 50cm). DTG/DTF pricing assumes 50% ink coverage; surcharge for heavy coverage.

Lead time (guide): 3–5 working days print-ready garments; 10–14 working days local manufactured apparel. T&Cs also quote 7–14 working days.
Shipping (ex VAT): R125 flyer (~15 tees) to R250 stock-6 box (~80 tees).
Artwork: high-res PNG with transparent background, or vector. No design service.
